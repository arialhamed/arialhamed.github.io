# What is Xteink??

So I was just on my phone on the train ride to work 2 days ago, and I was Shopee. I was supposed to be searching for something unrelated, but I saw the frontpage before it refreshed itself like it always does a second after loading.

The frontpage usually has all the ads and one listing that would more or less interest the user. Somehow, it interested me for once, because as I was able to catch it and see the listing, I saw that it was combination of 2 things that i've known of:
1. E-ink reader
1. Magsafe

I would've never known that these 2 would make such an interesting combo, so I thought, well, it could just be one of those one-offs that you'll see in the good ol' chinese market (like a lot of things on Aliexpress, Shopee & Taobao (even more so for Taobao)), but then I saw the reviews..

The second review that had media on it, the reviewer mentioned about something about Crosspoint, which seems to be some sort of firmware. I couldn't recall if i noted down the name of the brand of the listing so i saved the base concepts into a new tab in my phone that's definitely over a 100 tabs now.

When i got a break at work, i decided to search it up to see if there are legit offerings that i could get my hands on, and lo & behold: i found Xteink.

I was absolutely surprised to find that this Chinese company was somehow able to catch a notable online audience, especially Reddit of all places (it was even on their website as one of their social medias), and my gosh, it actually popped up like not too long ago, like around 2025 they started shipping their first product which was the first-gen X4.

---

So anyway, fast forward to 10th of Sept, I decided to get myself an X3. I chose the X3 particularly because of its size, and also, I am not sure if an X4 would fit any future phones that I may have, maybe smaller than my Pixel 8.

The only known post that I see fitting an Xteink to a Pixel 8 was this post right [here](https://www.reddit.com/r/xteinkereader/s/Z51OiJtWHo).
